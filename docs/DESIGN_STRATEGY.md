# OmniDoxa Design Strategy

## North Star

OmniDoxa should feel like premium editorial intelligence: serious, data-rich, calm, and visually distinctive. The design should support reading, comparison, and trust rather than marketing spectacle.

## Visual Principles

- Dark and light modes from day one.
- Editorial typography paired with precise UI/data typography.
- Clear hierarchy between Topic summary, Discourse Preview, and Premium Analysis.
- Locked premium panels should be visible but unreadable for non-Subscribers.
- Avoid generic SaaS card grids as the dominant experience.
- Use enough structure for scanning without overwhelming the reader.

## Key Screens

- Home Topic feed.
- Topic detail page.
- Admin queue and Editorial Review.
- Basic/Premium Briefing.
- Viewpoint Battle.
